02 / THE ARCHITECTURE MINDSET

Design the system.
Consider the whole picture.

Good cloud architecture connects business needs to the people who build, secure, and operate the solution.

01

Start with context.

Understand the workload, constraints, risk, and migration goals before selecting services.

REQUIREMENTS → DECISIONS
02

Make it repeatable.

Use infrastructure as code and clear deployment steps to reduce manual configuration.

AUTOMATION → CONSISTENCY
03

Plan for day two.

Consider access, observability, recovery, costs, and operational documentation from the beginning.
